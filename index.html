About Emma Carney

Stories of second chances, tangled hearts, and the lives we never saw coming.

Emma Carney is the contemporary romance and drama pen name of bestselling author Michael Parker. While Michael is celebrated worldwide for his pulse-pounding historical thrillers and high-stakes wartime epics, the Emma Carney pen name provides a luminous creative home for deeply emotional, character-driven love stories.

With an unflinching eye for human vulnerability and an innate sense of narrative pacing, Emma’s novels delve into the messy, tender, and uplifting truths of romance: unresolved childhood bonds, unexpected reunions, second chances at happiness, and the courage it takes to open your heart again.

All of Emma Carney’s novels — including Dare to Dream, Max and Emma, The Girl with No Name, Happy Lies the Heart, and Chapel Acre — are available worldwide in digital editions through Amazon Kindle, free to read with Kindle Unlimited, as well as in elegant paperback editions.
